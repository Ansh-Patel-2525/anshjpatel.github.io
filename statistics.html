How to choose a distribution

Bernoulli / Binomial

One yes/no trial, or the number of successes in a fixed number of independent trials.

Geometric / Negative binomial

The number of failures before the first success, or before a fixed number of successes.

Poisson

A count of events in a fixed interval when events occur independently at a constant average rate.

Normal family

A continuous, symmetric bell curve. The standard normal has mean 0 and variance 1.

Exponential / Gamma / Chi-squared

Positive waiting times and sums of them. Chi-squared is a special case of the gamma.

Uniform / Beta

Equal density over an interval, or a flexible density for a proportion between 0 and 1.

Lognormal

A positive quantity whose natural logarithm follows a normal distribution.
